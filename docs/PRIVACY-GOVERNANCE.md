# MapleWish Privacy Governance

Owner: Maple Vibe Inc.  
Privacy Officer: designated MapleWish privacy lead  
Public contact: https://maplewish.ca/contact.html (Privacy request)  
Policy version: 2026-09-19

This is an internal operating policy. It complements the public Privacy Policy and Applicant Privacy Notice.

## Core rules

1. Collect the minimum information reasonably needed for an identified MapleWish purpose.
2. Treat applicant stories, financial circumstances, disability/health context, photos and verification records as sensitive.
3. Limit sensitive applicant access to team members who need it for review or administration.
4. Never publish applicant information from the private application record without a separate publication approval.
5. Do not request government ID, medical records, banking credentials or diagnostic documentation at initial application unless a later verification need is documented.
## Retention schedule

- Abandoned application drafts: delete after 12 months of inactivity unless needed for security or a pending request.
- Declined or closed applications: delete or de-identify within 24 months of closure unless a documented legal, fraud, safety or dispute reason requires longer retention.
- Selected/approved assistance files: retain the minimum private evidence needed for program integrity and any applicable accounting/legal period.
- Financial, tax and transaction records: retain for at least the applicable statutory period; CRA business records are generally retained for six years from the end of the relevant tax year.
- Authentication sessions and expired verification/reset tokens: periodically purge after expiry and a short security-log interval.
- Public stories/photos: retain while the applicable publication consent remains valid and the public-history purpose remains active; honour withdrawal prospectively where legally and technically possible.
- Internal team notes: follow the retention period of the associated application and avoid unnecessary sensitive detail.
- Contact/privacy requests: retain while open and for up to 24 months after closure unless a longer period is needed to document legal compliance or resolve a dispute.

Document any exception and its reason.
## Access and verification

- Admin access must require authentication and should use least privilege.
- Do not copy applicant stories into chat, email, tickets or other systems unless necessary.
- When verification is required, record what was requested and why.
- Avoid storing full financial account numbers or unnecessary identity documents.
- If a document must be retained, restrict access and set a deletion date.

## Privacy requests

For access, correction, deletion, consent withdrawal or complaints:
1. Verify the requester sufficiently for the sensitivity of the request.
2. Record the request and date received.
3. Search relevant application, account, transaction, contest and audit records.
4. Provide or correct information as required, subject to lawful exceptions.
5. Record the response and any deletion/retention exception.
6. Escalate complex or disputed requests to the Privacy Officer.
## Confidentiality incident / breach response

On suspected loss, unauthorized access, disclosure or misuse:
1. Contain the incident immediately without destroying evidence.
2. Preserve relevant logs and identify affected systems/data.
3. Determine whose information was affected, sensitivity, volume, duration and likely consequences.
4. Reset credentials/tokens and close exposed access paths as appropriate.
5. Assess notification and reporting obligations under applicable federal/provincial privacy law.
6. Notify affected people and regulators where legally required, using clear mitigation guidance.
7. Maintain the required incident record and document the decision whether notification was required.
8. Complete a root-cause review and corrective actions.

## Publication approval

A public Wish should not be activated until:
- the need/recipient verification gate is complete;
- the exact public name/alias is approved;
- the exact public story is approved;
- the exact photo, if any, is approved;
- intended channels/uses are understood; and
- the approval is recorded in a durable form.

The admin “Story approved” control is a release gate, not a substitute for obtaining the recipient's actual approval.
