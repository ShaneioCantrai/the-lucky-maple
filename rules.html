Official Rules

Prototype placeholder — not final production contest rules.

Concept structure

The weekly promotional giveaway is intended for eligible Canadian residents age 18 or older. No purchase is necessary. Buying a leaf does not improve the odds of winning.

Prize

Concept prize: one $100 CAD prize per weekly promotional period, funded separately by The Lucky Maple and/or an identified sponsor.

Free entry

An eligible person may use the free online entry form during the applicable weekly promotional period. The production rules will define entry limits, opening and closing times, eligibility and verification requirements.

Selection and skill-testing question

A potential winner would be selected according to the final published rules and must correctly answer the required skill-testing question and satisfy all eligibility and verification requirements before being confirmed.

Marketing email

Contest entry and marketing consent are separate. The optional marketing checkbox is not required to enter the giveaway.

Before launch

The final rules must define the sponsor/promoter, geographic eligibility, complete dates and times, prize count and approximate retail value, odds language, entry limits, winner contact procedure, redraw procedure, privacy terms and all other required disclosures.
