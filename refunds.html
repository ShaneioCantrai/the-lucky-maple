Refund Policy

Effective September 19, 2026 refunds-2026-09-19

This policy applies to paid MapleWish leaf purchases and other consumer payments made directly through MapleWish.

1. General rule

Because a paid leaf is a digital item that may be created and displayed shortly after payment, completed purchases are generally final once fulfilled, except where a refund is required by law or where MapleWish determines a refund is appropriate under this policy.

2. Situations we will review

  • duplicate or accidental duplicate charges;
  • a technical error that charged an amount different from the amount authorized;
  • a payment made using a payment method without the authorized holder's permission;
  • a MapleWish cancellation or failure that prevents the paid digital leaf from being provided; or
  • another situation where applicable law requires a refund or remedy.

3. Wish changes

If a particular Wish closes, is completed or cannot proceed, the direct-help allocation may be redirected as described in the Contribution Terms. A change in recipient does not automatically create a refund right unless required by law or MapleWish specifically promised otherwise at checkout.

4. How to request a review

Use the MapleWish contact form as soon as reasonably possible and include the email used for the transaction, the approximate date and amount, and a short explanation. Do not send full card numbers or banking passwords.

5. Payment disputes

If you believe a payment was unauthorized, you may also have rights through your card issuer or payment provider. MapleWish may provide transaction records to the processor as reasonably necessary to investigate a dispute.

6. Mandatory rights

This policy does not limit any non-waivable refund, cancellation or consumer-protection right available under applicable law.
